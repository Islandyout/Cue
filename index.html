Documents, read aloud properly

Cue reads your document
like a script, not a list of words.

Drop in a report, plan or manual. Cue works out its structure, marks up the pacing, expands the numbers into words a voice can actually say, then reads it — slowing for headings, breathing between sections, leaning on what matters.

  • Word, PDF, Markdown, text, HTML
  • Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere
  • Tables are spoken as sentences, not as loose cells
